# Déjà Vu — stylised 3D art-direction prototype

Created October 12, 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool.

## Deliverables

- arena-concept.png: Aegis and armadillo versus Ember and phoenix, full-screen arena and minimal controls.
- companion-specials.png: Shell Charge and Phoenix Sweep visual studies.

These are static generated visual concepts, not gameplay captures, rigged 3D models, animation frames or production assets. The existing game has not been changed. The special labels are presentation concepts and do not establish new combat mechanics. Companion actions should map to existing authoritative special effects unless rules are deliberately revised.

## Art direction

Large sculpted full-body creatures, distinct silhouettes, three-quarter combat-facing poses, tactile armour/clothes, lavender and warm gold palette, grounded lighting and shadows. Arena-only active screen; history belongs after the match. Pets accompany specials without separate turns or health in the initial scope.

## Review notes

The arena has a strong visual hierarchy and clear silhouettes. The special study is more cinematic: its camera crop and oversized phoenix should be moderated for actual gameplay so both fighters and health bars remain readable. Generated energy gems and HUD are illustrative, not authoritative game UI; implement real controls as HTML/game UI rather than baking them into production artwork.

## Production next step

Approve or revise this look, then create separate environment, fighter poses/rigs, companion poses and effects. A single generated composite cannot serve as a fully animated battle. Preserve character consistency across those assets and bind presentation to combat events.
