# Character rules — accepted October 11, 2026

**Current direction — October 11, 2026:** Combat is now primary, explicitly superseding adventure-first requirements. The adventure contracts and milestones below remain preserved secondary functionality, not combat rules. Read [combat rules and build sequence](COMBAT_RULES.md) for the new versioned contract. Combat implementation status is recorded separately; prior test results do not establish combat functionality.

Exact implementation contract; tested completion evidence is in CHARACTERS.md. The user explicitly replaces equal-power characters with bounded holder advantages. Four starters have one signature charge. Eight holder classes have two; all begin with ten turns and one basic decoy, cap one. Rarity and NFT count never stack power. Any starter can finish the sandwich mission using existing non-ability solutions.

Each new run freezes character-definitions/1, ability-rules/1, sandwich-rules/2, sandwich-rooms/2, seed-parity/1, character ID and charge budget. Version 1 saves have no invented loadout and retain their original transition/policy. Actions, ability events, remaining charges and active effects are persisted and checked against deterministic replay. Zero-turn skills consume bounded charges; at most 32 recorded events, with ordinary turns still capped at ten.

Inspect mechanism is a separate one-turn clue interaction in v2. Route inspection and all old non-ability winning paths remain. Illegal skills spend nothing and return rejection. Each skill's target is an authored current-room interaction, not an arbitrary client-provided object.

General guest starts reject holder classes. Official selections/actions require the existing provider proof, chapter grant, fresh ownership and ownership generation. A published exhibit supplies its frozen loadout server-side for a guest challenge. Borrowed holder chapters cannot be republished as independent holder-class exhibits; their private journal remains usable. Personal guest races of a borrowed holder chapter stay bound to that published exhibit; withdrawal disallows new borrowed starts and strips public opponent/comparison access. Already-started private attempts may finish with their frozen resources, granting no general holder permission or official authority.

Ghost policy habit-abilities/2 sees permitted observations, legal choices, remaining charges and recorded room/ability support. Never train the pre-attempt opponent on its current attempt. Training and comparison stay within equal frozen loadouts/rules; publication derives its public ghost from the consented chapter alone. Separate copies protect lane resources. Exact action replay includes zero-turn skills and commit/cancel events; the spent-turn race clock coalesces zero-turn events, while exact replay preserves every event.
