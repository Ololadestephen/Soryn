## Measurement scope

[Exact file dimensions and byte measurements](evidence/animation-pilot/asset-measurements.json): Aegis 329,826 bytes and armadillo 273,586 bytes, **603,412 bytes together**. Their full 8-bit RGBA decode estimate is 12,581,568 bytes, approximately **12 MiB**. The five Aegis/Ember/environment files total 2,003,612 bytes compressed and approximately 30 MiB of full RGBA pixels. All fifteen runtime files total 6,478,002 compressed bytes and 94,357,184 RGBA bytes. These estimates do not measure browser heap, texture memory, SVG surface allocations or simultaneous residency.

[Browser observations](evidence/animation-pilot/browser-observations.json) record actual local resource transfer sizes/durations and animation-frame gaps on installed Chrome 155.0.8059.39, macOS 27.0.1 arm64, at a 390×844 viewport. The observer samples real `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks during existing playback. Its DOM logging and QA recording affect the measurement. Resource `decodedBodySize` is the HTTP body size, **not decoded image memory**. The route currently fetches selection art as well as active battle art; the resource list honestly includes it. This is local developer-machine evidence, without throttling, heap/GPU profiling or low-end-device validation. No Blender frame-rate/resolution/compression decision has been made.

The final observation has 347 samples: median 16.7 ms, p95 16.7 ms and maximum 33.3 ms. These are observed callback gaps during the fallback sequence, not an FPS guarantee or frame-render-time profile. Earlier QA observations varied up to 116.6 ms; one local run is insufficient evidence of universal smoothness. Existing compressed art bytes are unchanged, so no download-size improvement is claimed.
