# Human playtest readiness — October 12, 2026

No participants have been recruited or observed. This guide is prepared for user-authorized, consenting playtests; no outreach or personal-information collection occurred.

## Guest session (about 10 minutes)

1. Open `/arena`. Select one of Pip/Bolt/Patch/Moss, computer Bolt and **Easy**. Start without coaching. Read only the information visible in the game.
2. Try a basic, defence and legal special. After defence and the special, explain what happened to health, energy and cooldowns. Distinguish a shielded hit from health damage; describe any confusing symbol or timing.
3. Finish, inspect the result and choose Rematch. Confirm the setup remains the same. Use Change setup to try Medium and Hard with the same fighters.
4. Describe differences you actually noticed. There is no expected answer that Hard must always win; these policies are provisional. Try keyboard Tab/arrow/Space/Enter controls and a narrow viewport/reduced motion if relevant.
5. Do not publish anything or connect a wallet as part of this guest test. Adventures, ghosts and museum remain optional secondary experiences.

## Minimal feedback (no name, wallet, contact or personal information)

- Understanding: What did you think the three moves would do? What did they actually do?
- Visual clarity: Could you see who acted, each hit, block/dodge/counter, and the corresponding HP change?
- Tactical choices: Which move decisions mattered? Did any option feel consistently useless or dominant?
- Pacing: Which sequence felt rushed, slow or unclear?
- Perceived difficulty: What changed between Easy, Medium and Hard? Which matchup/move sequence was used?
- Rematch: Would you choose another match? What would you change first?
- Optional practical issue: device/viewport and normal/reduced motion; no identifying information required.

Record actual observations and uncertainty, not invented retention, satisfaction or human win rates. Sharing/collecting feedback outside this local task needs authorization.
